Separating content from presentation

Explanation

The strong and em tags are better choices than the b tag and i tag because they give text a meaning of what they do compared to only changing how the text looks. The strong tag shows that the text is important by bolding it. While the em tag emphasizes certain words in an italic way. In comparison, the b tag only makes text bold, and the i tag only makes text italic but does not have a term to represent it as. Using the strong tag and em tag also improves text the same way but also allows others who see the code to better understand the meaning and importance of the text.
